# Utilita Energy

> Utilita Energy is a UK household electricity and gas supplier (founded 2003, Eastleigh, Hampshire)
> specialising in smart Pay As You Go prepayment energy for roughly 800,000 mostly lower-income homes.
> It publishes NO developer programme, NO customer-data API and NO open market data. Its single
> public, anonymous, machine-readable API surface is its Atlassian Statuspage status API at
> status.utilita.co.uk, which reports the live health of Utilita's smart meters, payment channels,
> My Utilita app and portal, websites and contact centre.

Generated: 2026-07-27 by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline (generated — Utilita publishes no
llms.txt; https://utilita.co.uk/llms.txt returns 404).

## APIs

- [Utilita Status API](https://status.utilita.co.uk/api): Public, anonymous, read-only. Eight GET
  endpoints under https://status.utilita.co.uk/api/v2 returning page status, components, incidents
  and scheduled maintenance as JSON. No key, no sign-up, CORS-open.

## What does not exist

- No developer portal. developer., developers., api., docs. and data.utilita.co.uk do not resolve.
- No customer-data API. Usage, balance and top-up data live only inside My Utilita
  (https://my.utilita.co.uk/login) behind a customer account.
- No open market, grid or settlement data. GB open energy data comes from NESO (Carbon Intensity API),
  Elexon (BSC/settlement) and the DNO open-data portals — not from Utilita.
- No OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect, API keys, SDKs, CLI, Postman collection, MCP server, or GitHub
  engineering presence.
- No mandate compels any of it: Britain mandated smart-meter INFRASTRUCTURE (the Smart Energy Code and
  the Smart DCC), not a consumer data right. Third parties reach GB smart-meter data by acceding to
  the DCC as an "Other User" with consumer consent, not through the supplier.
